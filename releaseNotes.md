# Release Notes

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - July 23, 2026
- The very first release of Schengly, built to make Schengen trip planning feel easy and clear!
- Quickly check how many days you have left and stay on top of the 90/180-day rule without the stress.
- Add your trips, see your travel history, and get a clean overview of everything in one place.
- Export your trip list to PDF or CSV whenever you want a simple backup or a neat travel record.
